Collaboration & Resource Management

I bring client leaders, staffing, coding leads, auditors, and IT together to keep delivery on track across multiple healthcare accounts.

Cross-functional collaboration

I lead recurring client meetings, turn decisions into action items with clear owners, and follow up on access, workflow, and quality issues that can delay delivery.

Workforce and capacity planning

I match specialty skills and availability to workload, coordinate onboarding and replacement coverage, and adjust staffing with delivery leads as volumes and client priorities change.

Team support and performance

I partner with coding leads and auditors to review productivity and quality, clarify expectations, and coordinate coaching and follow-up when performance falls short.

How I manage accounts

I review revenue trends alongside staffing capacity and delivery performance. I compare recent results with the starting baseline and the highest recorded week to distinguish sustained expansion from a short-term spike. In client meetings, I use that context to discuss coverage needs, surface risks, and agree on next steps.
